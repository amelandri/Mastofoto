Transform a Mastodon list, in a beatiful clean photo feed!

Mastofoto shows you the posts from one of your Mastodon lists, but only the ones that include a photo: every text-only post is filtered out.

Connect your account, pick a list, and favourite or boost photos right from the feed.

Follow the project on Mastodon: @mastofoto@mastodon.uno

Connect your Mastodon instance

Some info about Mastofoto:
  • There is no server: Mastofoto is a static page that talks directly to your Mastodon instance from your browser. Nothing you do here is seen or stored by anyone else.
  • Your login credentials and access token are stored only in this browser's local storage, kept separate per Mastodon instance, and are cleared when you log out. Your chosen list is remembered even after logging out, ready again next time you sign back in.
  • Login uses Mastodon's standard OAuth flow with a random, one-time state value checked on return, so a login started here can't be hijacked or completed with someone else's authorization.
  • The app only ever requests permission to read your timeline and to favourite/reblog — it can never post, delete, or follow on your behalf.
  • Post content is run through an allowlist filter before being displayed, and only http(s) links are ever made clickable.
  • The page is served over HTTPS and sets a Content-Security-Policy restricting where scripts and styles may load from.
  • Because there's no backend, the app's own OAuth client credentials are visible in your browser — normal for a purely client-side app like this one, and not something that exposes your account or password.
  • Mastofoto is open source, released under the MIT license. View the source on GitHub →

    Version

    Mastofoto shows you the posts from one of your Mastodon lists, but only the ones that include a photo — every text-only post is filtered out. Connect your account, pick a list, and favourite or boost photos right from the feed.

    Features

    • Log in with any Mastodon instance — no server-side setup required
    • Choose which list to follow, with a preview of its members
    • See only posts (and boosts) containing photos, at full resolution
    • Favourite and reblog directly from the feed
    • New posts are highlighted since your last visit
    • Click any photo to view it full-size
