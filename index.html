Turn one Mastodon list into a beautiful, distraction-free photo feed. Connect your account, pick a list, and only the photos show up — ready to favourite or boost without ever leaving the feed.

Connect your Mastodon instance

🔒 Mastofoto is a static, single-user web app that lets you log into a Mastodon instance, pick one Mastodon list, and view only the posts in that list that contain photos (image attachments), with the ability to favourite and reblog them.

No information is stored on any server: this app talks directly to your Mastodon instance, and your access token stays only in your browser's storage.

It runs entirely client-side against the Mastodon REST API — there is no backend, no build step, and no database. All persisted state lives in the browser's `localStorage`. The page has no external dependencies besides the Mastodon instance itself.
